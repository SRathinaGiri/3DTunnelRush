Stereoscopic 3D Viewing Guide (v4.29.0)

This game supports 5 distinct rendering modes & WebXR VR for maximum 3D immersion:

  • 2D View: Standard single camera view with full-screen dynamic viewport auto-scaling.
  • Parallel 3D (Side-by-Side): Dual off-axis stereo camera rendering (Left eye image on left, Right eye image on right). Ideal for VR viewers, cardboard headsets, or parallel eye relaxation.
  • Cross-Eye 3D (Side-by-Side): Right eye image on left, Left eye image on right. Cross your eyes slightly until a middle 3D image appears in focus—no glasses required!
  • Smart Glasses 3D (HSBS): Widescreen 16:9 Half Side-by-Side 3D output custom-tuned for RayNeo Air, XREAL, TCL, and Rokid smart glasses optical engines!
  • Anaglyph 3D: Rendered via a dedicated 2-pass Red-Cyan shader filter. Wear standard Red/Blue 3D glasses!
  • 🥽 WebXR Immersive VR: Tap the VR button inside Meta Quest Browser to launch full 6DoF/3DoF immersive VR mode with native headset lens rendering!
  • Eye Distance (IPD) & Focal Sliders: Fine-tune camera separation anytime mid-flight for your personal eye comfort.

💡 Tip: If you experience eye strain or motion discomfort, reduce the IPD slider in settings for a gentler 3D depth effect.
