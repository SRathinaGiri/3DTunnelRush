Stereoscopic 3D Viewing Guide (v4.2.0)

This game supports 4 distinct rendering modes designed for maximum 3D immersion:

  • 2D View: Standard single camera view (500x500 viewport).
  • Parallel 3D (Side-by-Side): Left eye image on left, right eye image on right (1000x500 viewport). Use a VR viewer, cardboard headset, or relax your eyes straight ahead until the images overlap.
  • Cross-Eye 3D (Side-by-Side): Right eye image on left, left eye image on right (1000x500 viewport). Cross your eyes slightly until a middle 3D image appears in focus!
  • Anaglyph 3D: Rendered in Red-Cyan colors (500x500 viewport). Wear standard Red/Blue 3D glasses!
  • Eye Distance (IPD) Slider: Adjust camera separation for stronger or softer 3D depth effect.
